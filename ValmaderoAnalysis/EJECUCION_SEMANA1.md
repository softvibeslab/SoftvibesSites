## 📞 QUICK QUESTIONS?

**"What if I don't have video editing software?"**
- Use Canva (free) for video editing
- Or use CapCut (free, mobile app)
- Or use Instagram Reels editor (built-in)

**"What if my property photos aren't good?"**
- Use what you have
- iPhone photography is fine (natural light)
- Focus on story (copy + caption) not photography

**"What if I can't reply to all comments?"**
- Prioritize comments in first 2 hours (algorithm boost)
- 50 replies > 200 late replies
- Better to reply to 10 than none

**"Should I post all 3 pieces this week?"**
- YES. Consistency signals algorithm that account is active.
- Post at least 3 times per week (goal: 5-7)
- Stories 1-2x/day minimum

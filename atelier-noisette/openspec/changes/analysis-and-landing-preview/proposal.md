## Why

Atelier Noisette has a visible social identity and an artisanal-dessert offer, but a social profile requires prospective customers to reconstruct the catalog, occasion fit, ordering path, lead time, and delivery context from a feed. A focused private analysis and working landing can demonstrate a lower-friction owned conversion path without claiming access to private data or publishing unapproved business information.

## What Changes

- Create a private, evidence-backed analysis page using public Instagram and Threads observations.
- Create a responsive customer-facing landing concept centered on artisanal desserts, gifts, and celebrations.
- Use category-level product presentation and public-source imagery while current catalog, pricing, contact, location, and fulfillment details remain unknown.
- Route the preview CTA to the verified Instagram profile rather than inventing WhatsApp or order details.
- Keep both artifacts static, local, accessible, and free of forms, tracking, payments, or external writes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `atelier-noisette-analysis`: Explain verified current state, conversion gaps, proposed journey, evidence limits, and missing approval inputs.
- `atelier-noisette-landing`: Preview a warm, mobile-first ordering narrative with one safe conversion action.

## Impact

- New isolated directory: `SoftvibesSites/atelier-noisette`.
- No changes to Atelier Noisette accounts, content, links, customers, or infrastructure.
- Public-source images remain preview placeholders and require usage approval before publication.

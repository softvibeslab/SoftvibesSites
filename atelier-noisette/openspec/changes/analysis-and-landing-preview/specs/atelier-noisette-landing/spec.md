## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: One safe conversion action
The landing SHALL present one primary action that opens the verified Atelier Noisette Instagram profile until an approved order destination is supplied.

#### Scenario: Visitor wants to order
- **WHEN** the visitor activates a primary CTA
- **THEN** the verified Instagram profile opens in a new tab with an accessible label and no order is claimed as sent or confirmed

### Requirement: Occasion-first offer
The landing SHALL explain Atelier Noisette through broad, evidence-supported occasions without inventing current products, prices, or availability.

#### Scenario: Visitor scans the offer
- **WHEN** the visitor reviews the page
- **THEN** celebrations, gifts, and seasonal editions are legible without unsupported SKU or fulfillment claims

### Requirement: Responsive accessible presentation
The landing SHALL remain usable without horizontal scrolling, expose semantic headings and landmarks, provide visible keyboard focus, use descriptive image alternatives, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

#### Scenario: Narrow mobile viewport
- **WHEN** the landing is viewed at 390 CSS pixels wide
- **THEN** content and primary actions remain readable, reachable, and unobscured

### Requirement: Preview and media honesty
The landing SHALL identify itself as a proposal and SHALL state that public-source media and operational details require approval before publication.

#### Scenario: Visitor reaches the footer
- **WHEN** the footer is displayed
- **THEN** preview status and media/operational approval boundaries are explicit

### Requirement: Expanded public Instagram inspiration gallery
The landing SHALL include at least seven locally stored presentation derivatives selected from Atelier Noisette's publicly visible Instagram and Threads media, while preserving the evidence originals separately.

#### Scenario: Visitor explores visual references
- **WHEN** the visitor reaches the inspiration gallery
- **THEN** the gallery shows at least seven loaded images with descriptive alternatives and captions that frame the media as historical inspiration rather than current inventory

#### Scenario: Public media includes privacy or brand risks
- **WHEN** candidate feed media contains identifiable people, personal photographs, embedded promotional copy, or unrelated savory dishes
- **THEN** that candidate is excluded from the customer-facing gallery unless explicitly approved

## How To Borrow From It

- Use the section structure as a review checklist, not as a replacement for problem-first requirements.
- Use diagrams to clarify interfaces, state changes, and workflows.
- Keep requirements testable and traceable to stakeholder/customer problems.
- For DevhubVibes, add RACI, TRUST 5, ADR/spec links, and Android/MASVS considerations where applicable.
